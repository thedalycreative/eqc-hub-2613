**Date:** Wed 15 Jul 2026 · **Subject:** S2 (Front End Web Dev)

## Focus
Plan change! Before we clone anything there was a step we missed — Flexbox and containers. Full Flexbox lesson, three hands-on exercises, and a group playthrough of Flexbox Froggy.

## What we covered
- Flex container vs flex items; main axis vs cross axis
- `justify-content`: flex-start / center / flex-end / space-between / space-around / space-evenly
- `align-items` vs `align-content` — align-content only matters when items wrap
- The classic "center a div": `display:flex` + `justify-content:center` + `align-items:center` + `height:100vh`
- `flex-direction`, `flex-wrap` + `gap`, `flex-grow` / `flex-shrink`, shorthand `flex: 1`
- Three exercises: center a div · navbar · responsive card row
- The finale bug: one missing semicolon — the most common coding mistake there is
