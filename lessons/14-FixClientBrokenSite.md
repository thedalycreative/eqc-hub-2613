# Class 14 · Fix a client's broken site

**Date:** Wed 29 Jul 2026 · **Subject:** S1 (Clients & Concepts)
**Sub-title:** S01-A02 Part 2 · Marvin's Flower Delivery

## Focus
Subject 1, Activity 2 continues — Client B is **Marvin**, who runs an online flower delivery business. Half his site's links are broken. We diagnosed the problem, planned the fix, and rebuilt the missing pages live in class.

## What we covered
- **The symptom:** essential links on Marvin's site do nothing (navbar) or dead-end (footer) — every broken link is a lost sale
- **The diagnosis:** `href` attributes pointing at placeholder `#` values or filenames that don't exist in the folder
- **The fix plan:** full link audit → repoint every `href` to the correct relative path → build the missing `products.html` and `contact.html` → add a branded `404.html` as a fallback for the legal links
- Rebuilt **products.html** as a Bootstrap card grid (9 products) and **contact.html** as a Name/Email/Message form — matched the homepage styling exactly
- Wrote the client-facing "here's what I'm doing" email in the tone the SLA calls for — warm, plain English, no jargon
- Reviewed Marvin's **SLA**: acknowledge in 1hr · High-priority resolves in 2 business days · daily written updates · escalate before the deadline, never after
- Wrapped up with the follow-up feedback email (Q10) and zipped the fixed site for submission (Q11)
