Subject 1 · Clients & Concepts

S1.a02 · Provide ICT Support (Katie & Marvin)

Done · Classes 13 & 14 · One submission covering both clients.

The brief

Two small clients, two different ICT support tickets. Diagnose the problem, plan the fix, execute it, and communicate throughout — the way you would in a real support role.

  • Part 1 (Katie): Katie is a videographer. Her site can't be found on Google. The problem is SEO — the <head> is full of placeholder junk.
  • Part 2 (Marvin): Marvin runs an online flower delivery. Half his site's links are broken — navbar items go nowhere, footer links dead-end.

Tasks

Part 1 · Katie's SEO fix

  1. Rewrite <title>, <meta name="description">, <meta name="keywords"> and <meta name="author">.
  2. Match the metadata to the visible page content — consistency helps ranking.
  3. Copy the same metadata across every page.
  4. Zip and submit.

Part 2 · Marvin's broken site

  1. Answer all 11 questions in the activity brief.
  2. Audit every href across the site.
  3. Build the missing products.html, contact.html, and a friendly 404.html.
  4. Write the client-facing email in the tone Marvin's SLA calls for.
  5. Zip the fixed site and submit alongside the written answers.

Model answers

Reword, don't copy. Rewrite in your own voice before submitting.

Katie · what the fixed <head> looks like

<head>
  <title>Katie Reid Videography — Perth wedding & family films</title>
  <meta name="description"
        content="Warm, unhurried wedding and family videography in Perth,
                 capturing moments that last a lifetime.">
  <meta name="keywords"
        content="wedding videographer Perth, family video, event videography, WA">
  <meta name="author" content="Tim's EQC Minions · ICT Support">
</head>

Why this works: the title names the business, the service and the location. The description echoes the tagline visible on the homepage ("capturing moments that last a lifetime") — search engines reward that consistency. Keywords are honest and specific. The author is your company as the contractor, not the client.
