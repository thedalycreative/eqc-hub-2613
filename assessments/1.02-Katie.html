1.02 · Katie's SEO Fix

Subject 1, Activity 2, Part 1 — Provide ICT Support. Client A is Katie, a videographer whose site can't be found on Google. The problem is SEO, and it lives in the <head>.

The brief

  • Katie's existing site has placeholder junk in the head metadata ("Bruce Wayne", "hi I am a keyword")
  • Rewrite <title>, <meta name="description">, <meta name="keywords">, and <meta name="author">
  • Match the metadata to what's visible on the page — consistency is a signal search engines reward
  • The description should echo the homepage's tone (e.g. "capturing moments that last a lifetime")
  • author is your company (the contractor), not Katie
  • Copy the same metadata across every page — consistency is fine and recommended

Deliverable

Katie's site with corrected <head> metadata on every page. Zip the folder and upload to Study Hub → Subject 1 → Activities → Activity 2.

This is Part 1 of Activity 2. Part 2 (Marvin's Flower Delivery) is a separate submission.
