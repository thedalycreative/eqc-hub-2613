What you're assessing

ABC Solutions is a legacy business running on an HTML4/CSS2 website with no mobile support, no accessibility, and outdated hosting. Your job:

  • Diagnose the gaps in the current site against modern standards
  • Propose an HTML5 / CSS3 rebuild with cloud hosting
  • Cost the migration realistically (~$14,500 for the class model)
  • Reference Australian Privacy Principles and WCAG 2.1 compliance
  • Argue why an AI agent can do the HTML4 → HTML5 translation without a full rebuild
