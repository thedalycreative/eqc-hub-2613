Subject 1 · Clients & Concepts

S1.a05 · Lead and Facilitate a Team

Not yet run in class · Model answers are drafted below.

The brief

Give a short walkthrough of your build, take structured peer feedback, and pick the top three fixes to apply. The assessor is watching how you run the session — do you keep it on time, do you make quiet people welcome, do you turn feedback into action?

Tasks

  1. Give a 3-minute walkthrough of your current build.
  2. Facilitate 5 minutes of peer feedback using a shared rubric.
  3. Capture the feedback in writing — every comment attributed.
  4. Prioritise the top three fixes and commit to a "done by" date for each.
  5. Send a follow-up note to your peers thanking them and sharing your fix plan.

Model answers

Reword, don't copy. Rewrite in your own voice.

Q1 · Structure of a good 3-minute walkthrough

  1. 30s — Who this is for. Say the audience out loud. "This site is for first-time visitors deciding whether to book."
  2. 60s — The path. Click the exact route a real user takes to book. Don't tour the code — show the customer journey.
  3. 60s — One thing I'm proud of, one thing I'm stuck on. Naming the stuck point invites better feedback than "any thoughts?".
  4. 30s — Ask the room. One specific question.

Q2 · Peer-feedback rubric (5 lines)

  • One thing that works — be specific.
  • One thing I'd change first — be specific.
  • One question I couldn't answer from the site alone.
  • Accessibility: any obvious barrier I noticed?
  • Mobile: does the top of the page earn the scroll?

Q3 · Capture — the follow-up note format

Subject: Feedback from today — thanks + what I'm doing
Body: two-line summary of the feedback, three bullet points naming the top three fixes with dates, one sentence saying "I'll share the updated build in class on [date]".

Q4 · Prioritising three fixes

Score each piece of feedback on impact (1–3) and effort (1–3). Do the impact-3/effort-1 items first. If two things tie, do the one your peer flagged as blocking their comprehension of the site.

Q5 · Facilitation tips that show up in the mark

  • Keep an eye on the clock — the assessor is timing you.
  • Invite quiet peers by name before opening the floor generally.
  • Repeat the feedback back in your own words before writing it down.
  • Never say "yes but" — say "yes and" or "help me understand".
