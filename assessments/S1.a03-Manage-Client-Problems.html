Subject 1 · Clients & Concepts

S1.a03 · Manage Client Problems

Not yet run in class · Model answers are drafted below.

The brief

A client raises an issue with a live site you're supporting. Diagnose it, prioritise it against everything else on your plate, communicate a plan, and know when to escalate. This assessment tests the process around a fix, not the fix itself.

Tasks

  1. Write your team's SLA in plain English (response times, resolution targets, escalation path).
  2. Build a priority matrix (Critical / High / Medium / Low) with concrete examples of each.
  3. Draft the initial acknowledgement email a client sees when they raise a ticket.
  4. Draft the escalation email — client-facing — when a High-priority ticket is going to breach.
  5. Write a short post-incident note the client can read in 30 seconds.

Model answers

Reword, don't copy. Rewrite in your own voice.

Q1 · SLA in plain English

  • Acknowledge: we reply to every new ticket within 1 business hour.
  • Triage: we tell you the priority within 4 business hours.
  • Resolve targets: Critical = same day · High = 2 business days · Medium = 5 business days · Low = next scheduled release.
  • Comms cadence: at least one written update per business day until closed.
  • Escalation: if we can't hit the resolve target, we tell you before the deadline, name the blocker, and give a new date.
  • Closure: we don't mark a ticket closed until you sign off in writing.

Q3 · Acknowledgement email

Subject: Got your message — ticket #1234 opened

Hi Sam,

Thanks for flagging this — I've opened ticket #1234 and I'll come back to you with a priority and rough timing within the next 4 hours. If it turns out to be more urgent than that, I'll call.

No action needed from you right now.

— Tim · EQC Minions ICT Support

Q4 · Escalation email (before the breach)

Subject: Update on #1234 — target date shifting

Hi Sam,

Quick heads-up on ticket #1234. Our target was end-of-day Thursday. We've hit a snag with the payment gateway's API response — we're waiting on their support to confirm the change is on their end and not ours.

New target: close of business Friday. If that shifts again I'll email by 10am tomorrow with the reason and a new date. Nothing broken on your customer flow in the meantime.

— Tim

Q5 · 30-second post-incident note

What happened: The checkout page 500-errored between 2:14pm and 2:31pm Thursday. Cause: a payment-gateway timeout during their maintenance window (they didn't announce it). Fix: we've added a retry with a 3-second backoff so the customer sees a spinner, not an error. Impact: ~18 checkouts attempted during the window; 12 recovered on retry, 6 need a manual follow-up email — list attached.
